Privacy
SaveWise information
Welcome to SaveWise 🔴

Before you start, please review the Privacy Notice and Terms & Conditions. SaveWise is a personal budgeting tool and stores core financial records locally on your device.

Privacy Notice — summary

SaveWise is designed to keep core transaction and budgeting data on the user's device. Optional permissions, such as location, are requested only when needed for a feature such as timezone/location assistance. SaveWise should not collect precise location merely to display local time.

Terms & Conditions — summary

SaveWise provides budgeting and educational suggestions, not financial, investment, tax or legal advice. You are responsible for reviewing your records and decisions. Features may change as the app develops.

These are product-facing terms for the current prototype and should be reviewed by a qualified lawyer before commercial release.

Welcome to SaveWise 🔴

Before you start, please review the Privacy Notice and Terms & Conditions. SaveWise is a personal budgeting tool and stores core financial records locally on your device.

Privacy Notice — summary

SaveWise is designed to keep core transaction and budgeting data on the user's device. Optional permissions are requested only when a feature needs them. SaveWise should not collect precise location merely to display local time.

Terms & Conditions — summary

SaveWise provides budgeting and educational suggestions, not financial, investment, tax or legal advice. You are responsible for reviewing your records and decisions.
